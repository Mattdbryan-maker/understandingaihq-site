Read-only social analytics

Understand what your content is actually doing.

UNDERSTANDINGAIHQ Analytics is a private analytics and content-intelligence service used to review authorised social-account performance without publishing, messaging, or modifying account content.

How it works Privacy policy
01

Authorised access only

Accounts connect through the platform’s official authorisation flow. Access is limited to the permissions explicitly granted.

02

Read-only by design

The service is intended for analytics retrieval. It does not publish videos, send messages, manage comments, or alter account settings.

03

Evidence over guesswork

Platform-native metrics remain separate so reporting can respect the definitions and limitations of each source platform.

A lightweight analytics layer for UNDERSTANDINGAIHQ.

The service supports internal review of content performance, early signals, account statistics, and public-post analytics from authorised social accounts.

This site describes the analytics service and its data practices. It is not a public social-network product and does not sell access to user data.

Simple, explicit data flow.

The platform account remains the source of truth. The analytics service requests only the access needed to retrieve approved metrics.

1. AuthoriseThe account owner approves access through the platform’s official login flow.
2. RetrieveApproved profile statistics, public-content metadata, and engagement metrics are fetched.
3. StoreAnalytics data is retained only as needed for historical analysis and operational reliability.
4. ReviewRead-only reporting tools use the stored data to support content decisions.
