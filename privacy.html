Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UNDERSTANDINGAIHQ Analytics (“the service”) handles information when an authorised social-media account is connected for analytics purposes.

1. Purpose of the service

UNDERSTANDINGAIHQ Analytics is a read-only analytics and content-intelligence service. It is used to retrieve and analyse performance information from social-media accounts that have been explicitly authorised by their account owner or manager.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the platform and permissions granted, the service may process:

  • basic account identifiers and profile information supplied by the platform;
  • account-level statistics such as follower, following, likes, or content counts;
  • public-content identifiers and metadata;
  • content-performance and engagement metrics made available by the platform, such as views, likes, comments, and shares;
  • OAuth access or refresh credentials needed to maintain an authorised connection.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the analytics service, maintain historical reporting, diagnose sync reliability, and support internal content-performance analysis. The service is not designed to use connected-account data for advertising, profiling unrelated users, or automated publishing.

4. Read-only access

The intended integration is read-only. It does not use connected permissions to publish content, send messages, manage comments, or perform destructive account actions.

5. Storage and security

Analytics data may be stored for historical analysis and operational reliability. Authentication credentials are intended to be kept separate from reporting interfaces and protected using appropriate local credential-storage controls. Access is limited to authorised operators of the service.

6. Sharing and sale of data

Connected-account analytics are not sold. The service does not disclose connected-account data to advertisers. Data may be processed by the relevant social platform and infrastructure providers only as necessary to operate the authorised integration.

7. Retention and deletion

Analytics may be retained while needed for historical reporting. Authentication credentials may be retained while the connection remains authorised. If access is revoked, the service will stop using the revoked credentials. Data that is no longer required may be removed as part of normal operational maintenance.

8. Third-party platforms

When you authorise a connection, the relevant platform’s own privacy policy and developer terms also apply. UNDERSTANDINGAIHQ Analytics does not control how those platforms independently process information.

9. Your choices

You can revoke a platform connection through the relevant platform’s account settings or authorisation controls. Revoking access prevents future API retrieval using that authorisation.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project’s public GitHub repository.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service or platform requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
